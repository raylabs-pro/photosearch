PhotoSearch

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 August 2026 · PhotoSearch for Mac

PhotoSearch collects no data. Your photos, your searches, and everything the app learns about your library stay on your Mac.

What the app does with your photos

PhotoSearch analyzes the photos you give it access to — your Photos library and any folders you add — so you can search them by description. All of this analysis runs on your Mac, using machine learning models that ship inside the app. The search index it builds is stored on your Mac and never leaves it.

What we collect

Nothing. PhotoSearch has:

  • no accounts and no sign-in,
  • no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting of our own,
  • no advertising and no third-party SDKs,
  • no servers that ever receive your photos or searches.

The one network request worth knowing about

To turn a photo's GPS coordinates into a searchable place name ("rome", "beach"), the app uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which receives coordinates — never the photo, never anything that identifies you to us. This lookup is handled by macOS under Apple's privacy policy. Search itself works offline.

Purchases

PhotoSearch is distributed through the Mac App Store. Payment and receipt handling, if any, are performed by Apple; we receive no personal information from it.

Your data, your machine

Because everything lives on your Mac, deleting the app's data is entirely in your hands: remove a folder source in the app to drop its index, or delete the app to remove everything it built. There is nothing to request from us — we hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of PhotoSearch ever changes what happens with your data, this page will say so plainly before that version ships, and the change will be noted in the App Store release notes.
